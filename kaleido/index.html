DECIMEN — Kaleidoscope Symbology (EXPERIMENT)

The same fountain stream, drawn as a spinning mandala of colored wedges instead of QR codes — 3 bits per wedge, sync by rotation, per-frame color calibration ring. This page runs encoder and decoder in loopback (no camera) to measure what the symbology is worth; the camera path is future work. See docs/KALEIDOSCOPE.md.
Configuration
Innermost ring: rotation sync (the marker wedge's position IS the frame phase). Outermost ring: all 8 palette colors — the decoder rebuilds its classifier from it every frame. Noise adds ±N per RGB channel at sample time to probe classification margins.
